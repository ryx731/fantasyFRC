# Team theme references

Reviewed against team-owned branding on October 22, 2026:

- Citrus Circuits 1678: https://www.citruscircuits.org/ — citrus green with dark navy team shirts and white details.
- The Cheesy Poofs 254: https://media.team254.com/resources/254%20Identity%20Standards%20v2.2.pdf — page 13 specifies primary blue `#0070FF`, black `#232323`, and white `#FFFFFF`.
- Orbit 1690: https://www.1690orbit.com/ — deep blue and white header, logo, and titles. Replaces the unrelated orange palette.
- HighTide 4414: https://www.team4414.com/ — teal and deep ocean tones in the wave logo, with black and white details. Replaces the cyan palette.

Except for 254's published hex codes, colors are visual approximations of team-owned branding, not a claim of official hex specifications. Backgrounds, borders, muted text, and brighter link colors are UI adaptations for legibility. Primary buttons and preview swatches retain the team hues; semantic error/success colors remain distinct.

Scope theme variables to `html[data-theme=…]`. Selector buttons use `data-theme-choice` so preview controls never inherit a different page palette accidentally. Themes remain saved per browser as before.
